HOW TO READ THE RESULT

Policy is evidence. Sending guidance is stricter.

DO NOT SENDStrong lookalike evidence or a blocking engine policy. Verify through a trusted out-of-band channel.
VERIFY FULL ADDRESSWarning state, or engine ALLOW without the complete low-risk RPC contact evidence required for a clear display.
PREFLIGHT CLEARDisplayed only when engine policy is ALLOW, risk is explicitly LOW, a real risk index exists, and RPC contact evidence was collected.

Recipient Shield is technical decision support. It does not accuse a wallet owner of fraud or wrongdoing, and it does not replace independent confirmation of a material transfer destination.
