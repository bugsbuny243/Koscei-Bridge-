Customer workflow

One scan. Four questions answered.

Koschei keeps the underlying ARVIS collectors and deterministic evidence contracts. The customer enters one investigation and receives a connected security case rather than a pile of unrelated scanner cards.

01 · DecisionShould I proceed?

Allow, review, withhold or block stays tied to evidence rules where a decision is available.

02 · CauseWhat changed the decision?

03 · ExposureWhat could happen?

Supported attack paths and transaction consequences remain hypotheses until the evidence proves more.

04 · EvidenceCan I verify it?

Technical evidence, source, confidence and limitations remain available for independent review.
