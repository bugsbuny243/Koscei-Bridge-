Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026

Scope. This policy applies only to the Koschei Lang application presented through its Pi / Railway application host. It does not replace the separate privacy policy for Koschei Web3 / ARVIS on tradepigloball.co.

1. What Koschei Lang is

Koschei Lang is a developer-tool project focused on capability-secure local software execution. The Pi application is used for product access, product information, authentication and Pi payment flows where enabled.

2. Information we may process

  • Pi account information that you authorize Pi SDK to share with the application.
  • Pi payment identifiers, transaction identifiers, payment status and related metadata needed to approve, complete, reconcile or support a purchase.
  • Basic technical request data such as timestamps, application errors and security logs needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting support.

3. What we do not ask for

Koschei Lang does not ask for your wallet seed phrase, private key, wallet password or device unlock credentials. Do not send those credentials to us.

4. How information is used

Information is used to operate the application, authenticate permitted users, process and verify Pi transactions, issue or restore product entitlements, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, improve security and comply with applicable obligations.

5. Payments and Pi

Pi authentication and Pi payment functionality are provided through Pi Network infrastructure and are also subject to Pi Network's own terms and privacy practices. Koschei Lang does not custody user wallets and does not request or store wallet private keys.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared with infrastructure providers only as necessary to host, secure and operate the application, or when required by law.

7. Retention and security

We retain operational and transaction records only for as long as reasonably necessary for product access, accounting, dispute handling, security, legal or fraud-prevention purposes. We use technical and organizational controls intended to reduce unauthorized access, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may stop using the application at any time. Where applicable, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information that we control, subject to records we must retain for security, transaction, legal or accounting purposes.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as Koschei Lang moves from Testnet preview to commercial release. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Operator

Koschei Lang is operated by TradePI Global. Support and privacy requests may be submitted through the support channel displayed in the application or its Pi listing.
