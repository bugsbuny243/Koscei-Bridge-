PUBLICATION BOUNDARIES

What this registry does not claim

The public UI preserves the evidence boundary instead of upgrading technical correlations into real-world identity or intent.

A dossier existing internally does not make it public. Explicit publication is required.
Bundle, verdict, and evidence rows are not rewritten for the showcase. The UI adds presentation only.
On-chain relations do not become a real-world identity, intent, wrongdoing, rug, or same-operator claim without canonical evidence authority.
Owner secrets, customer accounts, private scan history, and internal worker details never belong in the public response.
