Deliver watchlist evidence without changing verdict authority.

Enterprise webhook management sends existing Koschei watchlist alert payloads to customer HTTPS endpoints through the persistent delivery queue. Delivery transports existing watchlist alert evidence; it does not create, upgrade, or rewrite a verdict.
